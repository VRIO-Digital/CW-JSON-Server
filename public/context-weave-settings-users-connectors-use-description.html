              Cradle-to-Grave Compliance & Liability Intelligence — VLS Texas Molecular (Deer Park)
            


            

              Use description
            

          


          
        


        


          

            Framing.
            VLS-as-operator. VLS runs a RCRA-permitted Class I injection TSDF in Deer Park.
            Every load it accepts carries the generator’s liability with it. This use case
            unifies the inbound manifest stream, each generator’s and transporter’s federal
            compliance record, and public enforcement documents into one queryable model so
            VLS can see the risk it is taking on — before it accepts the next load.
          


          

            Scope.
            Deer Park facility only. VLS Texas Molecular is always the receiving facility.
          


          

            Problem.
            Manifest data, generator compliance history, and enforcement documents live in
            three disconnected places (BigQuery e-Manifest views, RCRAInfo, and PDF consent
            decrees). No one can answer ‘which of the generators shipping to us is under
            active enforcement, and how much waste did we take from them last quarter?’
            without a manual, multi-day cross-reference.
          


          

            Not building.
            This demo does not unify VLS’s internal EAM, LIMS, or ERP systems — that is the
            narrated roadmap, shown as greyed future connectors, never faked.
