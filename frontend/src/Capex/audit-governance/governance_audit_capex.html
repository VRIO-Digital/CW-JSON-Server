⛨ Audit & Governance

One place for who sees what. Authors decide who can open a report or scenario when they publish it; you decide which projects each person can see — and every open is logged.

✉Who can open
Set by the author at publish time, by email address. Managed per report and per what-if in the first two tabs.
◱What they see inside
Data access per person, managed right on the reader's row — open “Manage access” on any report or what-if, pick the field the restriction runs on — project, region, business unit, budget category, compliance driver — then the values they may see. It follows the person everywhere.
☰Every open is logged
Who opened what, what their access resolved to, and every access change — in the audit trail.
Every event keeps who, what, when, and what the reader's access resolved to. Nothing here is editable — the trail is the record. Cost caps, the sealed predicate digests behind each trace, and the declared-aggregate rule are not on this screen; they are in governance_audit_data.json.
Exploration only — demo data. Northline Water Group, 60-project CAPEX portfolio. Every count on this screen is resolved against that roster at paint time, never stated. Not wired to the live graph.
